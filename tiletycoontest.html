How To Play

  1. You begin with three Level 1 Houses.
  2. Click a tile to select it.
  3. Click another tile to perform an action.
  4. Matching tiles merge into the next level.
  5. Clicking an empty space moves the tile there.
  6. Different tiles swap places.
  7. Buy additional Level 1 tiles using cash.
  8. Higher-level tiles produce more cash.
  9. Level 15 is Graycen's Level.
  10. There are 20 levels total.
  11. If you somehow merge two Level 20 tiles, the game will ask you an important question.
